## Creating/Overriding Achievements

Geometry Dash handles its achievements in the `AchievementManager` class. To create custom achievements, you may hook the following function:

```cpp
#include <Geode/Geode.hpp>

using namespace geode::prelude;
```

For each achievement, ensure you replace the following:

- `[Achievement Name]` with your achievement's name (try make it short and fun)
- `[Level Name]` with your level's name
- `[myfangame]` with the name of your fangame (alphanumeric only), e.g. `surge`
- `[ICON UNLOCK]` with the icon you would like to unlock - way to type this is below
